Chordae is a longitudinal heart-health companion that brings scattered pieces of cardiovascular care into one continuously updating profile, instead of making people rely on separate one-off tools. Rather than a single risk score or a generic pamphlet, it turns a person's numbers, family history, symptoms, habits, and recovery progress into one connected, updating as their life and choices change, and always framed as a starting point for a doctor conversation rather than a diagnosis. 

KEY FEATURES:

Plain-language risk explainer  :  Converts basic health metrics (blood pressure, cholesterol, age, lifestyle factors) into a clear, personalized explanation of cardiovascular risk, grounded in an established risk framework rather than an invented score.

Family history mapper  : lets you add relatives' heart conditions, since genetic risk is easy to overlook, and shows how it affects your own risk-a factor most people never think to track.

Symptom Journal: A simple tool to track subtle, easily missed warning signs—especially non-traditional symptoms common in women. Smart AI detects patterns over time and highlights what you should share with your doctor. 

"What-if" lifestyle simulator : Interactive sliders showing the concrete impact of specific habit changes (e.g., reducing sodium, quitting smoking) on the same risk picture, instead of generic advice. 

Recovery tracker : For those recovering from a cardiac event, tracks prescribed milestones and explains what each stage of recovery means, reducing uncertainty about whether healing is on track.
